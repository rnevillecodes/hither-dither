HitherDither

A lightweight desktop app for inspecting WAV files: waveform, format and levels at a glance, with editing and VST3 plugin support when you need them.

Download Other platforms

Version 0.3.0 · free for Windows, macOS and Linux

What it does

See the whole file

A fast waveform with an overview strip, format metadata, and peak, RMS and clipping statistics per channel. Play the file, or just a selection of it.

Many files, one window

Each WAV opens in its own tab. Drop files on the window to open them, and your tabs come back the next time you start the app.

Quick edits

Crop, delete or silence a selection, add fades and crossfades with draggable handles, and undo anything. Edits show up straight away, even while playing.

VST3 effect chains

Run each tab through a chain of VST3 effects, heard live with their own editors. A level meter shows peak and RMS after the chain.

Your original stays put

HitherDither never changes the WAV you open. Work is saved as a small session file holding your edits and every plugin's settings.

Export when ready

Render edits and effects to a new WAV, or swap in a new take with Replace Audio and keep the effect chain you dialled in.

Opening unsigned builds

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so your OS warns you the first time.

  • Windows: if SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: right-click HitherDither in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. On newer versions, use System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
  • Linux: make the AppImage executable first with chmod +x.
